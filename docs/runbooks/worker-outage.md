# Worker outage

Check `/health/live` first, then `/health/ready` and the separate `todir_worker_postgres_available`, `todir_worker_rabbitmq_available`, `todir_worker_successful_cycle_available`, and `todir_worker_successful_cycle_age_seconds` signals. A live process with readiness `503` is not processing-ready; do not infer consumer or scheduled-job health from Outbox polling. Inspect the durable Outbox backlog, MassTransit transport state, consumer failure counter, and consumer progress signals without sending probe messages. Restart or replace the worker from the same immutable image, verify it resumes claims with tenant context, and inspect duplicate/idempotency and poison-message counters. Do not bypass the Outbox or acknowledge work before the consumer transaction is durable.
